How the recommendation score works

The app creates preference weights from your genres, AniList tags, and animation studios. Rated titles are judged relative to your own average, while completed/current/rewatched/dropped statuses provide smaller implicit signals. Candidate tags are weighted by AniList's tag relevance. A title also gets a strong franchise boost when it is a sequel or related work to something you watched and liked. AniList average score and popularity are deliberately smaller tie-breakers. The resulting 0–100 number is a personal match score, not an objective review score.

Everything runs locally in your browser. The only network calls are GraphQL POST requests to https://graphql.anilist.co plus cover images served by AniList's image CDN. This public-profile version does not send or store an AniList password or OAuth token.
